# HOLYBERG editorial website

## Direction and reference
The user approved an editorial redesign inspired by Substance, after rejecting the previous decorative motion pass. The live reference at https://substancetm.framer.website/ was inspected in Chrome on 15 November 2026. Its computed display sizes were 96–112 px with tight tracking, secondary mono labels 11 px, warm white #edebe6, near-black #0a0a0a and large-scale photography with substantial negative space. Borrow the scale hierarchy and editorial composition for HOLYBERG's own images and content.

## Identity and tokens
HOLYBERG green #5aba3f, canvas #050304, warm paper #edebe6, muted #a5a49e, neutral rule #343432. The original extracted HOLYBERG logo, mountain art and press-kit photographs remain the visual assets. Holyberg Press is the uppercase display type. Body uses system sans-serif at 17–20 px, 1.5 line-height. Navigation and section labels use system monospace at 11–12 px. Display sizes range from 34 px on phone to 100 px on wide desktop, with a 1120–1440 px content measure.

## Composition
Full-width green cover with black original logo and mountain silhouette, compact top navigation, collection identity and a scroll link. Manifesto on black with large green statement and a wide documentary image. Alternating editorial spreads combine readable text with portraits. A full-width red photograph announces the visual identity section. Installation photographs form a deliberate staggered two-column gallery. A large contact link closes the page.

## Behavior
Document scrolling with section links and a compact menu after the cover. The sticky archive follows document scroll, with one active media item in front of neighboring layers. Categories jump to the start of each group. Exit rewinds the stage before continuing to Events or the visual introduction. Photos open at full size, and videos start in the same native dialog after a press. Escape, close button and backdrop dismiss the viewer with focus restoration. The red image expands as it enters view, and headings and selected photos move independently of body copy. Reduced motion shows the archive as a regular grid with category jumps. Without JavaScript, media links and the game link still work.

## Responsive contract
Below 760 px, editorial columns stack, display typography scales down, and reading copy remains at least 16 px. At 819 px and below, the sticky archive uses a compact category select, 44 px controls and the same perspective depth projection as desktop, with 46vw media and a 0.78 horizontal spread to fit the narrow screen. Media use smaller display assets where appropriate and retain the original photo for the viewer. The game opens beneath contacts, with a poster that stays visible until the embedded game's first rendered selection screen. Rotating while the game is visible anchors the viewport to its frame through layout reflow, preserving iframe focus without reloading the game. Rotation elsewhere leaves document navigation alone.
